SeaWindow is designed, built and supported by one person: Mohamed Elatabany, an independent iOS developer. I started it because checking the tide before a trip to the coast meant juggling a tide table, a weather app and a moon calendar. SeaWindow puts the tide curve, the next turn, the weather on the water and the sky on one screen.

How the app is made

  • Sources you can check. Every tide names its station, distance, provider and datum. US predictions come straight from NOAA CO-OPS. Outside the US the app calculates tides on the iPhone from published station constants, uses WorldTides, or falls back to the EOT20 global model and labels the result an estimate.
  • Privacy first. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your places and journal stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
  • Honest about limits. SeaWindow is a planning aid, not a navigation or safety tool. Predictions do not include storm surge, wind or river flow.

How this site is written

Guides and tools on this site are written by me. Numbers in worked examples come from NOAA CO-OPS and other named sources, retrieved on the date given in the guide, and app features are checked against SeaWindow’s source code and its live App Store listing. Prices are not shown here because they vary by country; the App Store shows yours before you subscribe.

If you find a mistake, please email a correction. I fix errors and update the date on the page.
